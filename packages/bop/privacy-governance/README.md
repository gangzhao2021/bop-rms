# `@bop/privacy-governance`

Cross-cutting, runtime-inactive Privacy Rights Request coordination for WP-2146. It tracks the four
accepted rights, proportional verification, owner-scoped work, legal holds, encrypted single-use
exports and minimal tombstones. Data remains owned by its source Domain; Privacy coordinates
through ports and never queries or edits another Domain's private tables.

No raw contact, Customer name, export content, legal conclusion, production identity proof,
persistence, migration, Job, real erasure or external artifact is included. Required financial,
Audit and Compliance facts are never physically removed by this package.
