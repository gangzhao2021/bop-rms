# ADR-0013 — Fleet / Route Optimization

- Status: `Proposed`
- Owner: Delivery Product Owner
- Affected modules: Delivery, Maps, Workforce
- Source: Canonical Handoff Section 80.7, version `0.5.3`

## Context

Delivery Tasks and provider adapters are sufficient for the current baseline.

## Decision

Do not implement an owned fleet or route-optimization platform now.

## Consequences

Batch routing, optimization KPIs, and workforce expansion are excluded until committed.

## Revisit trigger

An owned fleet, batch routing, or route-optimization KPI is committed, before the fleet Phase.
