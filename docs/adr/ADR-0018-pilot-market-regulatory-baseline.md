# ADR-0018 — Pilot Market and Regulatory Baseline

- Status: `Accepted`
- Owner: Product Owner + Compliance Owner
- Affected modules: Tax, Payment, Privacy, Hosting, Localization, Operations
- Decision date: 2026-07-15
- Source: Canonical Handoff Sections 80.7 and 81, version `0.5.3`

## Context

The first production-pilot market had to be selected without claiming unavailable Provider or legal execution evidence.

## Decision

Use Canada as the first production-pilot baseline. China remains a supported architecture and later market profile.

## Consequences

Pilot planning follows the Canada baseline; this ADR does not create accounts, credentials, registrations, legal facts, or production resources.

## Revisit trigger

The business owner explicitly changes the first production market before an irreversible Provider or hosting commitment.
