# ADR-0014 — Digital Signage Domain

- Status: `Proposed`
- Owner: Product Owner
- Affected modules: Menu, Media, Device
- Source: Canonical Handoff Section 80.7, version `0.5.3`

## Context

Menu presentation and device output cover current needs.

## Decision

Do not create a Digital Signage Domain now.

## Consequences

Scheduled multi-screen content and proof-of-play are not implied by menu/device output.

## Revisit trigger

Scheduled multi-screen signage content and proof-of-play are required, before a signage WP.
