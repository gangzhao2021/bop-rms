# ADR-0025 — Pilot Legal Entity Provenance

- Status: `Accepted`
- Owner: Product Owner + Finance / Compliance Owner
- Affected modules: Operating Entity, Tax, Payment, Compliance
- Source: Canonical Handoff Sections 80.7 and 84, version `0.5.3`

## Context

The pilot legal-entity provenance must be separated from unrelated existing entities.

## Decision

Plan one new dedicated Ontario corporation for the v0.1 Pilot; do not reuse an unrelated existing entity.

## Consequences

This is a planning decision only and does not incorporate a company or create legal facts, accounts, credentials, or filings.

## Revisit trigger

Before incorporation, professional due diligence supports a different provenance.
