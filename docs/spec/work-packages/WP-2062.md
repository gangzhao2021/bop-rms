## Resolved local boundary

WP-2062 adds a deterministic fail-closed edge and ingress policy gate. Customer, Merchant and
integration traffic terminate only at the Canadian regional ALB under regional WAF; the separate
CloudFront distribution is limited to content-hashed non-PII public assets from private S3 through
OAC. Private, Provider and `/api/v1/public/*` responses are excluded from CloudFront.

The policy pins the accepted ALB and CloudFront TLS policies, ALB desync/header/timeout/forwarding
controls, exact-origin CORS, staged WAF enforcement and registrar/DNS/certificate launch gates. It
stores no hostname, account identity, credential or deployed-resource fact.
