## Owning facts and boundaries

Reservation & Waiting owns a Store / area / service Capacity Policy draft and its published or
scheduled immutable version, local time buckets, Reservation capacity and online allocation,
controlled Manager-only overbook allowance, closures, effective period, and references to Pricing-
owned deposit / cancellation / no-show policies. Dining owns actual Table compatibility and live
availability; Store owns operating hours; Pricing owns fee amounts and terms; Payment owns money
movement. Capacity Policy never assigns a Table or creates a Reservation.

## Acceptance contract

1. Capacity Policy create / revise / publish / schedule are strict idempotent Commands with Tenant,
   Brand, Store, Actor, purpose, `dining.operate`, exact expected version, append-only revision / Audit
   evidence and no client-authored external facts.
2. Buckets are non-overlapping, bounded local wall-clock intervals with integer capacity, online
   allocation and explicit Manager-only overbook allowance. Closures and effective periods are
   validated; published versions are immutable and correction creates a new draft revision.
3. Demand / conflict simulation is deterministic from an explicit scenario and owner-issued Dining,
   Store, Reservation / Hold and Pricing-policy evidence. It reports blockers / warnings and inputs;
   it never creates a hold, promises capacity, assigns a Table or calculates a fee.
4. `RES-CAPACITY-CONFIG` implements all Section 88 fields, date / area / service filters, draft,
   simulation, publish / schedule actions and mandatory states. Mutation controls stay visibly
   disabled without an authorized command-capable BFF.
5. Handoff Section 50 authorizes no Reservation schema or migration namespace. Persistence,
   publication, cross-domain adapters and live projection remain injected ports; this WP must not
   invent a database, Event Catalog entry, legal policy, Provider or Store fact.
