## Owning facts and boundary

Pricing continues to own versioned Price Books, Price Records, the canonical eight-level scope
priority and exact integer-minor-unit resolution. Catalog supplies public Sellable facts; Tenant and
Store supply scope membership. No Catalog/Store private table is read and no client amount becomes
authoritative. A Price Book is single-currency and `Draft | Published | Archived`; published versions
and operation history remain append-only.

## Public contract

- Commands: create/replace Draft, validated bulk import intent, simulate coverage/resolution,
  submit/approve/publish, schedule by effective period and archive.
- Writes require Tenant/Brand, Actor, purpose, `pricing.price-book.manage`, Expected Version,
  idempotency, fact validation, UTC time and append-only Audit. Publish additionally requires
  `pricing.price-book.approve`, a different authorized Actor from the Draft author and a complete
  conflict-free scenario matrix.
- Events: `PriceBookDraftCreated:v1`, `PriceBookDraftReplaced:v1`,
  `PriceBookVersionPublished:v1` and `PriceBookArchived:v1`, committed with the operation/Audit
  adapter and consumed idempotently by `pricing.price-book-admin-projection:v1`.
- Queries: `query.price_book_list`, `query.price_book_editor`, `pricing_price_book_admin_v1` and
  `pricing_price_book_entry_v1`; projection generations/checkpoints are replayable and Brand scoped.

## Screen contract

`PRICE-BOOK-LIST` at `/app/commerce/pricing` exposes Product/SKU/code search, Store/channel/currency,
active/future/missing/conflict filters, source/effective period/coverage fields and create/import/
coverage/compare/schedule/archive actions. `PRICE-BOOK-EDITOR` at `/app/commerce/pricing/{id}` exposes
scope hierarchy, Sellable prices, integer-minor money, effective periods, rounding/tax references,
conflict rows and history plus edit/import/simulate/review/approve/publish actions.

Both pages implement loading, empty, permission denied, not found, feature disabled, stale, conflict,
command failed and offline read-only states. Without an authorized BFF, commands are visibly disabled.
Section 88.4 and 88.27–88.30 accessibility/responsive/privacy rules apply.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Coverage analysis distinguishes Covered, Missing and Conflict without changing canonical priority.
2. Commands enforce Brand scope, permission, Expected Version, idempotency, exact public facts,
   append-only Audit and an atomic minimal event.
3. Publish requires complete conflict-free coverage and four-eyes approval when policy applies.
4. Projection persistence keeps integer-minor money, event linkage, rebuild checkpoints, forced Brand
   RLS and no PUBLIC grants.
5. Both Screens expose all registered fields/actions/states and reject markup, URLs and numeric money.
