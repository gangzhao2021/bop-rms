## Owning facts and boundaries

Procurement owns Purchase Requisition identity, requested lines, approval, PO allocation
relationships, explicit remainder cancellation and closure. Inventory may create only a Draft through
the accepted public Need handoff using Need ID as its source/idempotency key. Purchase Order remains a
separate Aggregate; this WP may allocate approved quantities only to validated PO Draft lines and
cannot approve or issue a PO. Actual receipt remains Inventory-owned.

## Acceptance contract

1. Model Brand-scoped Purchase Requisition as a stable Aggregate Root with requesting Store/entity,
   requester, urgency, required-by date and append-only lines referencing Inventory Items, exact
   decimal quantities/units and zero-or-more Need sources.
2. Keep workflow (`Draft`, `Submitted`, `InReview`, `Approved`, `Rejected`, `Cancelled`), allocation
   (`NotAllocated`, `PartiallyAllocated`, `FullyAllocated`) and closure (`Open`, `Closed`) independent.
3. Enforce Draft revision, explicit submit/review/approve/reject/cancel Commands, Expected Version,
   reason where required and requester/approver segregation. Approval is internal work only and
   never an external Supplier commitment.
4. Append allocation relationships from Requisition Line to validated PO Draft Line. Support split
   allocation, reject over-allocation, and never alter approval when allocation changes.
5. Close only when every requested line quantity is fully allocated or its remaining quantity is
   explicitly cancelled/waived with reason and required approval. Receipt facts never affect status.
6. Revalidate exact Tenant, Brand, requesting scope, Requisition/Line version, Item/unit and approved
   Offering/PO-Draft allocation result. Reject any result claiming PO approval or issue.
7. Define strict `procurement_requisition_v1` list/detail projection with Brand + requesting
   Store/entity scope, 30-second freshness, cursor pagination, registered filters and permission-
   trimmed amount estimates, approval identities and allocation references.
8. Implement canonical `PROC-REQUISITION-LIST` and `PROC-REQUISITION-DETAIL` routes with all fields,
   stale/partial read-only behavior, omitted unauthorized actions and adjacent blocking reasons.
