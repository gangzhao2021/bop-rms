# WP-2239 - Atomic Cart Quote Attachment Store

## Authority and scope

Owner authorizes sustained implementation and verified local commits. Baseline073d1fb, branch
codex/wp-2239-cart-quote-store. WP-1203/1204, WP-2225 stable replay and current authorization
govern immutable Quote attachment to an exact Cart version. Composite authority is unchanged.

Ordering persists its attachment and line evidence through existing owner tables, never Pricing
private SQL. Pricing remains authoritative for Quote facts. Lock the Cart, verify active lifecycle,
exact version/line evidence and Audit, then append attachment, lines and public Audit atomically.
Amounts travel to/from PostgreSQL as canonical decimal strings and become bigint internally.
Attaching a Quote does not mutate Cart, item, lifecycle or version facts.

The service verifies loaded Cart identity and Session scope before returning version/lifecycle
information or invoking Pricing. Replay verifies the exact operation. Concurrent same-key callers
may produce distinct immutable Quotes; one attachment wins and both return that original result,
with current authorization and bounded historical intent validation. No second Audit or deadline
renewal. Existing quote/idempotency expiries and unknown-outcome failure contracts remain intact.
