# WP-2172 — Inspection / Finding / Corrective Action / independent verification

## Authority and scope

- Handoff Sections 40.1, 40.4, 40.7–40.10, 40.18, 40.22–40.25, 40.29, 46.6–46.7,
  48.5, 50.20–50.23 and 88.16 define the Compliance ownership boundary, append-only Inspection,
  Finding and Corrective Action records, independent Verification and the canonical screens.
- Exact local predecessor: verified WP-2171 commit `d11c642`.
- `@rms/compliance-food-safety` owns the policy and immutable record contracts. Product, Inventory,
  Kitchen, Store, Device and other business Domains continue to own operational actions; Compliance
  records only opaque owner-issued Action/Event outcomes.
- Section 50 grants no Compliance schema or migration namespace. This WP adds no database object or
  runtime adapter and does not claim durable production operation.

## Acceptance contract

1. Inspection records pin Case, Tenant/Brand/optional Store, closed Inspection Type, checklist and
   Requirement Version, stable Scope snapshot, inspector/authority references, scheduled/started/
   completed instants, internal score/rating code, regulator rating code, immediate-action and
   follow-up codes, Evidence references and immutable record/revision versions.
2. Inspection lifecycle is closed to `Draft | Scheduled | InProgress | Finalized | Cancelled`.
   Finalized facts never update in place. A correction is an append-only revision linked to the
   preceding revision and a bounded correction reason; it never rewrites the source record.
3. Findings have stable identity, exact Section 40.8 Severity and Status, pinned Requirement
   Version, immutable revision history and opaque Evidence/action references. Critical or Immediate
   Danger emits explicit escalation fact. `AcceptedRisk` is prohibited for a Hard Requirement and
   requires authorized expiry/review evidence for an advisory requirement.
4. Corrective Actions remain Case-owned entities with stable identity, Finding, required-action
   code, owner, due date/timezone, priority, exact Section 40.9 status, completion Evidence,
   owner-Domain outcome, verifier/result and immutable assignment/revision history. `Completed` is
   not `Verified`; overdue state escalates but never fabricates completion.
5. Verification results are exactly `Passed | PassedWithConditions | Failed | UnableToVerify`.
   Critical/Immediate Danger or Hard Requirement actions reject same-actor verification. Failed
   verification returns the Action to `VerificationFailed`; conditional pass pins condition,
   expiry and review time; unable-to-verify remains nonterminal and task-linked.
6. Every mutation revalidates Tenant Context, Actor, purpose, action-specific Permission, exact
   scope, expected Case version, idempotency and safe Audit evidence. Dependency uncertainty,
   stale version, cross-scope references and malformed/restricted content fail closed.
7. Only the six accepted Section 40.29 Finding/Action Events are published. Inspection changes do
   not invent Events. Event payloads contain safe Case/record references and no Finding text,
   Evidence bytes, employee/health/allergy facts, authority content or arbitrary free text.
8. `CMP-INSPECTION` and `CMP-CORRECTIVE-ACTION` expose Section 88 fields, filters, permission-gated
   actions and explicit loading/denied/offline/stale/conflict/error/empty states. UI parsers accept
   safe codes/references/counts only; Evidence and regulator source content remain out of contract.
