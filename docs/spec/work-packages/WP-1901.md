## Contract and ownership

Payment remains sole owner of attempt/transaction state, captured/refunded amounts, Provider truth
and reconciliation. The Projection accepts only strict Store-scoped public snapshots with safe
internal references, exact version/digest and canonical instants. Money uses non-negative decimal
minor-unit strings in CAD; binary floating point is absent. It enforces refunded <= captured <=
authorized and rejects inconsistent Refunded, Matched and Difference states.

Provider `Unknown` and reconciliation `Pending` are retained, never upgraded from age or Payment
state. A Difference requires an exception reference; Matched requires Provider Confirmed. Every
generation exposes Business Date, checkpoint, as-of/projected/rebuilt time, initialized-empty and a
5-second Fresh/Stale target. Stale reads cannot authorize capture, cancel, refund or matching.
Durable Projection Builder storage and shadow switching remain WP-1904.
