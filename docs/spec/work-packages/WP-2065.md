## Resolved local boundary

WP-2065 adds a deterministic fail-closed cloud-operations evidence contract. It pins the accepted
CloudWatch/ADOT/X-Ray path, encrypted least-privilege alert transport, privacy-safe role routing,
Budgets/Cost Anomaly/quota controls, bounded capacity signals and a real-contact staging
acknowledgement drill gate. Cost alarms are notify-only and cannot delete data or stop transactions.

The repository stores no contact, subscription, topic/account identity, Customer payload, threshold
claim or provider delivery result. Existing transport-neutral observability contracts remain the
local source of alert, telemetry and failure-isolation behavior.
