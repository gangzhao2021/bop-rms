# WP-2145 — Communication History and Template governance

## Authority and scope

- Handoff Section 33.13 requires Notification to check current Consent and Preference immediately
  before send and separates necessary operational communication from Marketing. Section 88.14
  defines `COMMS-HISTORY`, `COMMS-TEMPLATE-LIST` and `COMMS-TEMPLATE-EDITOR`.
- Accepted WP-1720–1724 already own Provider readiness, escaped receipt rendering, immutable attempts,
  authorized resend, suppression and privacy-safe SES outcomes. This WP adds administrative contracts
  and pages without reopening those decisions.
- Notification owns requests, templates, routing, attempts and suppression. The implementation is
  runtime-inactive and port-backed because no accepted Notification persistence namespace exists.

## Acceptance criteria

1. History rows distinguish Operational and Marketing, expose masked/opaque recipient evidence,
   immutable template/source/attempt facts, suppression and bounded Provider state.
2. Resend is allowed only for eligible Operational history through the accepted authorization and
   idempotent append-attempt boundary; Marketing cannot use operational resend.
3. Template identity has append-only locale/channel/purpose versions, required variables, escaped
   approved preview fixtures and a tracking prohibition.
4. Publish/archive require Expected Version, exact permission, distinct approval and atomic audit;
   prior delivered content remains pinned to its historical Template Version.
5. Strict permission-trimmed pages implement the canonical filters/actions and become read-only on
   stale or partial projections.
