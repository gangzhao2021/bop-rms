## Purpose

Define Payment's provider-neutral, per-Attempt `payment-terminal-capture-watchdog:v1`
application contract. It consumes strict Payment-owned Terminal authorization evidence and an
Ordering-owned acceptance evidence boundary, captures a non-Interac authorization immediately
after Order acceptance with a stable Provider key, raises the ten-minute operational alert, treats
fifteen minutes as the capture target and enforces the twenty-minute or verified shorter Provider
deadline. This Work Package is contract-first: current Terminal and Order-acceptance producers,
runtime adapters and scheduler do not exist and are not invented or claimed as production evidence.

## Closed execution contract

1. Payment owns watchdog policy, capture/cancel orchestration, safe results and capture-deadline
   exception requests. Ordering alone owns Order acceptance. Task owns Task lifecycle. The Provider
   adapter owns Stripe translation. Payment never reads Ordering or Task private persistence and
   never changes an Order or treats Task completion as Payment truth.
2. Job identity is `payment-terminal-capture-watchdog:v1`. Each per-Attempt command has an exact
   operation reference, Brand, Store, Payment Attempt, System or named Actor, purpose
   `WatchTerminalAuthorization` and scheduled UTC instant. Parsing accepts only closed plain data;
   no client amount, status, Provider object, deadline or control key is accepted.
3. Authorization precedes exact operation replay, lease, source, Ordering, Task and Provider reads.
   The same operation and digest returns its committed result; changed scope or content conflicts.
   An Attempt-scoped fenced lease prevents concurrent capture/cancel work. One authoritative
   injected clock instant is read only after authorization, exact replay and lease; caller-carried
   `scheduledAt` never selects a timing branch. Ordering eligibility must be no later than that
   evaluated instant. Provider and Task receipts produced by the ensuing calls may be later than
   the evaluated instant but no later than the verified fence expiry; a receipt beyond the active
   fence fails closed and leaves retry to reconciliation. If a run evaluated before the hard
   deadline receives an ensuing Provider observation at or after that deadline, it starts no new
   financial mutation: it records the due Task/exception intent and returns reconciliation for a
   fresh scheduler run whose authoritative clock is at or after the deadline. Provider time is not
   substituted for the Payment claim/Audit clock.
4. Payment authorization evidence is immutable and exact: authorization, Intent, Attempt, Order
   and Batch references; Brand/Store; safe Provider account and intent references; `Test | Live`;
   `TerminalCard | TerminalInterac`; `ManualPreferred`; positive CAD requested/authorized Money
   and non-negative captured Money; normalized status; authorized instant; nullable verified Provider
   capture-before instant; positive version and SHA-256 digest. A capturable card candidate is
   `TerminalCard + ManualPreferred + Authorized` with equal requested/authorized amounts and zero
   captured amount. Interac is single-message `Captured`, never `Authorized`, and has no separate
   capture deadline. Mutable, accessor, extra, stale, impossible or cross-scope evidence fails
   closed.
5. Ordering adds one strict public immutable `OrderAcceptanceEvidence`: acceptance and checkpoint
   references, positive source version and digest, exact Brand/Store/Order/Batch/Payment Attempt,
   `phase: Accepted`, `acceptanceKind: TerminalAuthorization` and accepted UTC instant. WP-1309
   adds no acceptance Command, lifecycle mutation, Event, table or producer. Payment revalidates
   every identity and time field and must never alias Submitted, PaymentPending or OrderCreated as
   Accepted.
6. Interac short-circuits before Ordering lookup, mutation claim, capture/cancel/retrieve, Task or
   exception and returns bounded `InteracSingleMessage`. The adapter's existing rejection of an
   Interac authorization or separate Interac capture remains defense in depth.
7. The ten-, fifteen- and twenty-minute watchdog thresholds all anchor on authoritative Payment
   `authorizedAt`: `alertAt = authorizedAt + 10m`, `targetCaptureAt = authorizedAt + 15m` and
   `policyDeadline = authorizedAt + 20m`. `hardDeadline` is the earlier of that policy deadline and
   a verified Provider capture-before instant. Exact Ordering acceptance is capture eligibility,
   not a second clock: capture begins immediately when both facts exist. `now >= hardDeadline` is
   the inclusive hard stop. A shorter Provider deadline wins every threshold; effective alert and
   target are clamped to it. Future, non-canonical or impossible time evidence fails closed.
8. Before acceptance and before the hard deadline there is no Provider mutation and the bounded
   result is `AwaitingOrderAcceptance`; the ten-minute Task rule still applies to the orphaned
   authorization. After exact acceptance and before the hard deadline, Payment first atomically and
   idempotently claims a stable capture operation plus Restricted Audit evidence, then invokes
   `CaptureIntent`. At or after the hard deadline clause 11 supersedes the waiting state even when
   acceptance never arrived: the outstanding authorization must be retrieved and safely resolved,
   not left open forever. The permanent Provider key binds environment, Attempt, purpose, amount
   and scope; a changed replay conflicts.
9. A capture response is operation evidence, never authoritative terminal truth. After every
   capture return, failure or thrown/unknown outcome the service retrieves Provider truth and
   strictly verifies context, intent, method, capture mode, CAD amounts and observation time. Only
   `ProviderRetrieval`, or the unchanged verified-webhook path outside this service, may enter the
   existing terminal sink. Captured retrieval records idempotent success; Failed/Cancelled
   retrieval records bounded failure; Pending/Unknown/unavailable remains unresolved.
10. At or after the effective alert instant, if retrieval has not proven Captured, Payment ensures
    exactly one Store-scoped public `@bop/task` receipt from an Attempt-stable intent. It strictly
    revalidates `TaskRecord`: source `PAYMENT_ATTEMPT` with exact Attempt and digest, task type
    `TERMINAL_CAPTURE_WATCHDOG`, `CRITICAL` severity and priority, `Assigned | Claimed`, Queue
    assignment and `dueAt = max(hardDeadline, task.createdAt)` so a late first observation never
    constructs an already-invalid Task. Task or optional Notification failure cannot undo, replace
    or become Payment truth; retry uses the same intent and returns the original Task.
11. At or after the hard deadline the service never starts capture. It retrieves first. If exact
    truth is still Authorized and there is no unresolved mutation race, it claims one permanent
    audited Cancel operation, invokes `CancelIntent` and retrieves again. Mutation responses alone
    remain non-terminal. Retrieved Captured/Cancelled/Failed truth is handed idempotently to the
    terminal sink; Pending/Unknown/unavailable remains unresolved and is never guessed.
12. Every Attempt not proven Captured by the hard deadline requests one stable Open Critical
    capture-deadline exception composition receipt with reason `CaptureDeadlineExceeded`, exact
    Brand/Store/Attempt, evidence digest and opened instant. This is an injected WP-1309 boundary,
    not an assertion that WP-1307's existing `PaymentReconciliationException` or SQL constraint can
    already persist the new reason; the runtime adapter and canonical exception projection remain
    gated for WP-1310. It is not disguised as a WP-1307 comparison reason. Confirmed cancellation,
    failure and unresolved outcomes all retain the receipt so the deadline breach is visible.
    Unknown remains `ReconciliationRequired`; WP-1310 alone owns late paid-but-unfulfillable
    compensation and refund reconciliation.
13. The strict immutable result exposes only
    `AwaitingOrderAcceptance | PendingCapture | CaptureConfirmed | CancelConfirmed |
ReconciliationRequired | InteracSingleMessage`, evaluated instant, nullable
    alert/target/hard-deadline instants and nullable safe Task/exception references. Interac returns
    all threshold and receipt fields as `null`; every card result has all three threshold instants.
    Errors are limited to
    `PAYMENT_TERMINAL_WATCHDOG_INPUT_INVALID | PAYMENT_TERMINAL_WATCHDOG_PERMISSION_DENIED |
PAYMENT_TERMINAL_WATCHDOG_LEASE_UNAVAILABLE | PAYMENT_TERMINAL_WATCHDOG_OPERATION_CONFLICT |
PAYMENT_TERMINAL_WATCHDOG_SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE |
PAYMENT_TERMINAL_WATCHDOG_DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE` and one safe message. Missing acceptance is a
    normal waiting result, not an error.
14. The WP-1308 new-work Kill Switch is intentionally absent: watchdog work is in-flight truth and
    recovery and must continue while new Provider admission is blocked. Webhook, terminal and
    reconciliation behavior remains append-only and unchanged.
15. Capture, cancel, Task, exception and terminal keys are stable per Attempt and purpose. A commit
    failure after an external effect cannot authorize a new financial mutation; retry first
    reconciles Provider truth and exact receipts. All content or ownership conflicts fail closed.
16. Task data contains only an opaque Attempt source, type, severity, due time, status and digest.
    Raw Provider evidence, PAN/CVV/client secret, Order/customer/staff/reader/provider references,
    amount and unnecessary PII are prohibited from Task, public error, logs, URLs and analytics.
    Metrics may use only bounded operation/outcome/reason/deadline buckets and never Tenant,
    Payment, Actor or Guest identifiers as labels.
17. This WP adds no Stripe SDK, HTTP/UI, Event, table, migration, real reader/location, credential,
    external resource, production Provider call or deployment. The injected source, operation,
    lease, Task, exception, terminal and scheduler adapters express required durability but do not
    claim a current runtime implementation.

## Risks, rollback and boundaries

- Risk: Submitted/OrderCreated is treated as acceptance. Mitigation: only the Ordering-owned exact
  `Accepted` evidence parser is eligible and its actual producer remains explicit.
- Risk: a timeout produces a second charge or cancel race. Mitigation: permanent per-Attempt action
  claims and Provider keys plus retrieval-before-decision and retrieval-after-mutation.
- Risk: Task state or a Provider mutation response becomes financial truth. Mitigation: both are
  operational evidence only; terminal facts accept verified webhook or Provider retrieval.
- Rollback: remove the contract/service and manifest/dependency/job declarations. No database or
  financial fact rollback exists because this WP introduces no persistence implementation.
- External Evidence: actual Stripe API/SDK pin and account keys; Terminal Location, approved reader
  and named Staff flow; `manual_preferred`, Interac and in-person refund matrix; real Provider
  capture limits and network unknown behavior; real scheduler/lease; production Terminal and
  Order-acceptance producers; Task/reconciliation persistence adapters; Permission/Audit grants;
  telemetry/incident drills; PCI/entity/compliance evidence all remain gated and unclaimed.
- Authorization boundary: local code, synthetic tests and repository documentation only. No
  credential, Provider/cloud mutation, deployment or non-local data action.
