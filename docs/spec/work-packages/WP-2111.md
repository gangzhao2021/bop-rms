## Owning facts and boundaries

Kitchen owns the Store-scoped Production Batch plan, execution lifecycle, actual yield observation,
quality hold and exception. Recipe supplies a published version-pinned production definition;
Inventory supplies eligible Item / Lot references and owns stock, movement and valuation. Kitchen
records controlled consumption evidence and emits a minimal fact for Inventory; it does not mutate
Inventory private tables, rewrite Recipe, infer food-safety approval or claim a physical count.

## Acceptance contract

1. Create Plan, Start, Record Yield / Consumption, Complete and Quarantine are explicit idempotent
   Commands with Tenant, Brand, Store, Actor, purpose, permission, Expected Version, append-only
   Audit and atomic minimal Events.
2. Planned and actual Yield / Ingredient quantities are bounded integer microunits. Recipe version,
   station, Inventory Item and optional Lot references are pinned; floating point and client-entered
   cost are forbidden.
3. Completion requires positive actual Yield, complete consumption observations and no quality hold.
   A variance above the injected policy threshold requires an append-only quality exception.
4. Quarantine preserves prior production and consumption facts, records a structured reason and
   does not claim disposal, stock adjustment or regulatory disposition.
5. `KIT-PRODUCTION-BATCH` implements every Section 88 field, filter, action and mandatory state;
   without an authorized BFF mutation controls are visibly disabled.
