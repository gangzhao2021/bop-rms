## Closed orchestration contract

The existing logical Notification idempotency key remains purpose + business source + recipient
reference + locale + Template Version. One logical Notification may have append-only Delivery
Attempts. An Accepted attempt is terminal. `Unknown` is never blindly retried: a new attempt requires
an exact authorized-resend evidence reference and at least 15 minutes. Rejected attempts also require
authorization and use bounded backoff. At most three attempts are admitted; exhaustion is
Dead-lettered for owned operational disposition rather than silently retried.

Suppressed routes never attempt delivery. Delivery observability exposes only Notification and
Attempt opaque references, channel, sequence, bounded state and timestamps. It excludes recipient,
destination, rendered body, Provider payload, resume URL/token and business detail.

  Suppressed without private fields;
