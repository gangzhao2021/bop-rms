# WP-2144 — Consent and Contact Preference evidence

## Authority and scope

- Handoff Section 33.13 separates Brand-specific Contact Preference from append-only, purpose- and
  channel-specific Consent evidence. Section 33.15 requires `CustomerConsentChanged`; Section 88.14
  defines `CONSENT-PREFERENCE` at `/app/customers/consents/:customerId`.
- Identity owns actual email and phone values. This WP stores opaque Contact Method references only
  and does not turn Loyalty enrollment into Marketing consent.
- No accepted Customer & Loyalty persistence namespace exists, so the implementation is
  runtime-inactive and port-backed. It does not invent a migration, live Event publication result,
  legal policy, jurisdiction determination or verified proof.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Consent choices append immutable Granted/Withdrawn facts by purpose, channel, Contact Method,
   policy version, jurisdiction, source, Actor, recorded/effective time and evidence reference.
2. Latest status is derived deterministically; withdrawal immediately denies new Marketing use and
   cannot be replaced by an older-effective choice.
3. Contact Preference is a distinct Brand-scoped versioned record for language, preferred channel,
   quiet hours, frequency and Store preference; it is not authorization.
4. Operational communication classification is evaluated separately from Marketing consent, and
   Notification receives a current decision contract rather than raw contact data.
5. Commands require Tenant, Brand, Customer, purpose, permission, expected version, idempotency,
   verified proof and audit/Event evidence through explicit ports.
6. The canonical page strictly parses a permission-trimmed projection and supports record verified
   choice, withdraw and proof export without exposing raw Identity contact values.
