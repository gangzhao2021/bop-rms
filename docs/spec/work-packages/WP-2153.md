# WP-2153 — Delivery handoff / proof and Customer-safe tracking projection

## Authority and scope

- Handoff Sections 34.59–34.62 and 34.69–34.80 define append-only Delivery Proof, pinned proof
  policy, dual-confirmed Store-to-Courier custody handoff, attempts and completion evidence. Section
  88.6 defines `CUST-DELIVERY-STATUS` at `/orders/:orderReference/delivery`.
- This WP extends `@rms/fulfillment` with runtime-inactive handoff/proof contracts and adds a strict,
  Customer-safe PWA tracking projection. No real location, courier identity, proof asset or Customer
  authorization is invented.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Courier pickup validates current Accepted assignment, complete ready packages/items, seal, Staff and
   courier/provider confirmation; missing/conflicting custody evidence yields review, never Picked Up.
2. Proof is append-only and policy-version pinned. Only a fully `Validated` proof may authorize Delivered;
   technical uncertainty is Pending/Needs Review rather than Rejected.
3. Proof assets/signatures/photos remain references with integrity hashes and retention/classification;
   restricted/high-risk hard requirements and self-approved override fail closed.
4. Customer tracking exposes coarse status, bounded ETA, safe handoff/proof summary and support path only;
   it excludes courier contact, raw location, address, proof asset, Provider payload and internal IDs.
5. Exact Order-authorized Guest Session, feature, freshness and cutoff evidence gate access and instruction
   update; stale/partial/offline views are read-only.
