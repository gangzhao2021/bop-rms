## Owning facts and boundaries

Procurement owns Brand-scoped Supplier identity, lifecycle, contact/address references and Supplier
Qualification Entities. Supplier Offering, price, Requisition, Purchase Order and performance facts
remain separate Aggregates or projections. Inventory and Compliance may collaborate only through
public references, decisions and summaries; no cross-Domain private-table access is allowed.

## Acceptance contract

1. Create `@rms/procurement` with Supplier as an independent Brand-scoped Aggregate Root. Stable
   identity and history survive suspension, inactivity and archive; physical delete is absent.
2. Enforce lifecycle `Draft`, `Active`, `Suspended`, `Inactive`, `Archived`: suspension/inactivity do
   not mutate historical POs; controlled reactivation is required; Archived restores only to
   Inactive before a separate reviewed activation.
3. Model Supplier Qualification as stable Entities with append-only versions: type, jurisdiction,
   certificate/licence number, issuer, Effective Period, document reference, review status and
   Supplier/Offering/Item Category scope. Renewal never overwrites prior evidence.
4. Required qualification expiry blocks new Offering publication and PO issue through public
   eligibility results. It never cancels an issued PO or blocks necessary receipt. Emergency
   override remains excluded without an approved policy/approval contract.
5. Define strict `procurement_supplier_v1` list/detail projections with Brand scope, 30-second
   freshness target, stable cursor pagination, registered filters and authorized summaries for
   Offering count, open PO count and performance. Projection failure never fabricates zero facts.
6. Authorize before query, idempotency, repository, policy, impact or audit access. Contact,
   address, certificate/licence and evidence fields are purpose/permission-masked and cannot be
   searched except by an exact approved contact reference.
7. Every command requires Actor, purpose, permission, operation reference, expected version where
   applicable and atomic Audit. Replays validate Tenant/Brand/Supplier ownership and exact intent.
8. Implement canonical `SUP-SUPPLIER-LIST` and `SUP-SUPPLIER-DETAIL` routes with the Section 88.24
   state matrix, permission-trimmed actions and stale/partial read-only behavior.
