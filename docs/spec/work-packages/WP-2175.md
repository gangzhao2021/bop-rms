# WP-2175 — Allergen Review / Food Safety Incident / Publish and Payment Block Linkage

## Authority and scope

- Handoff Sections 40.1, 40.14, 40.17–40.19, 40.22–40.25, 40.29, 87.11.2 and 88.16,
  plus accepted WP-1028, WP-1407 and WP-2027, define source ownership, structured allergen review,
  incident privacy, immutable snapshots and owner-executed safety blocks.
- Exact local predecessor: verified WP-2174 commit `c6fd8ff`.
- Catalog/Recipe own Ingredient, Recipe, Product/SKU/Option declarations, provenance, publication
  validation and availability. Kitchen owns exact Order configuration review and operator
  acknowledgements. Ordering/Payment own Checkout/Payment admission. Compliance owns control
  verification and Food Safety Incident/Case facts, but invokes operational blocks only through
  explicit owner ports and records opaque outcomes.
- Section 50 grants no Compliance schema or migration namespace. This WP adds no database object or
  runtime adapter and does not claim durable production operation.

## Acceptance contract

1. Allergen Control Review records pin Tenant/Brand/Store, stable review/revision identity,
   exact Ingredient/Recipe/Sellable-path subject and configuration digest, applicable Allergen
   Policy and Recipe/source versions, controlled allergen references/classifications, supplier
   Evidence references/status/expiry, reviewer, result and immutable recorded time.
2. Source classification is closed to `Contains | CrossContactPossible | Unverified`; absence is
   never inferred. Review status is closed to `Pending | Approved | Rejected | Invalidated` and
   Evidence status to `Current | Expired | Conflicting | Unverified`. Approval requires exact,
   current, non-conflicting source versions and never creates an allergen-free claim.
3. Source/configuration change invalidates the prior review through a new revision. Unverified,
   expired, conflicting, rejected or invalidated facts fail closed and may request Catalog
   publication/availability and Ordering/Payment admission blocks through explicit idempotent owner
   ports. Compliance never directly publishes, unpublishes, changes availability or writes Payment.
4. Food Safety Incident records pin a Compliance Case, exact Incident type, severity, occurrence /
   report times, structured Store/Product/Order/Lot/Employee scope, restricted allegation/health
   snapshot references, containment, Evidence, notification, investigation and outcome references.
   Reports do not establish liability and preserve immutable Order/Recipe/handling snapshot digests.
5. Incident status is closed to `Reported | Contained | Investigating | CorrectiveAction |
Verification | Closed | Cancelled`. Closing requires current Verification and owner outcomes;
   refund, compensation, recall, disposal, closure and operational blocks remain owner actions.
6. Committed failure/incident facts publish only `AllergenControlFailureDetected` and
   `FoodSafetyIncidentReported`. Events contain safe scope/record/requirement/severity/time only;
   they exclude medical narrative, Customer/Employee identity details, Evidence bytes and Payment.
7. Every mutation revalidates Tenant Context, purpose, action-specific Permission, exact scope,
   expected revision, idempotency and safe Audit evidence. Stale/cross-scope source or Case facts,
   incomplete restricted references, dependency uncertainty and unauthorized owner actions fail
   closed. Mandatory allergen/health and Immediate Danger blocks are never manager-overridable.
8. `CMP-ALLERGEN-REVIEW` and `CMP-INCIDENT` expose Section 88 fields/actions and explicit
   loading/denied/offline/stale/conflict/error/empty states. Strict UI contracts accept safe
   codes/references/digests/counts only and never raw Evidence, symptoms/allegations, contacts,
   Customer notes, employee PII, legal narrative, credentials, arbitrary text or URLs.
