## Resolved local boundary

WP-2060 adds a deterministic fail-closed account and identity baseline: management carries no
workload; security/log archive and three workload environments are isolated; production never shares
development data, KMS keys, Secrets or roles; human access is short-lived Identity Center with MFA;
root and long-lived-key controls are explicit; SCPs deny the accepted unsafe actions; break-glass is
two-person, time-bound and centrally alerted.

The repository stores no account ID, root contact, credential, Actor or deployed policy. Synthetic
policy evidence does not claim an AWS Organization exists.
