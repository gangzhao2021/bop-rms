## Owning facts and boundaries

Procurement owns the Supplier Discrepancy case and PO-fulfillment response. Inventory remains the
source of immutable Receipt, accepted/rejected/damaged quantity and Stock Movement facts. Resolution
references public Inventory correction/Void events or explicit PO remainder actions; it never edits a
Receipt, Ledger movement, cumulative received quantity or Supplier performance metric.

## Acceptance contract

1. Create one idempotent discrepancy from a scoped Goods Receipt line event for every explicit short,
   over, rejected, damaged or quality variance, preserving immutable PO/Receipt/Event source facts.
2. Model append-only acknowledge, assignment, supplier-contact outcome and resolution decisions with
   Expected Version, Actor, time, controlled reason and Audit. Source quantities never change.
3. Accept overage only when the issued PO tolerance snapshot proves it is allowed. Request correction
   or replacement through public Commands without implying completion.
4. Waive/cancel a short remainder only with an independent approval and an exact Procurement-owned PO
   line outcome. A Receiver cannot self-approve the waiver.
5. Close only after every required resolution reference is present and the current public PO/Receipt
   projection confirms no pending correction, replacement or remainder action.
6. Define strict `procurement_discrepancy_v1` with PO/Receipt/Supplier search; type/status/Store/owner/
   overdue filters; separately masked Supplier contact, evidence, cost and history fields.
7. Implement `PROC-DISCREPANCY` `/app/supply/discrepancies` with acknowledge, assign, policy accept,
   correction/replacement request, approved remainder waiver and guarded close; stale/offline is read-only.
