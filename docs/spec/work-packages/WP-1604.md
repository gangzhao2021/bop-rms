## Bounded outcome

WP-1604 owns the stable public `FulfillmentCompleted.v1` fact and its append-only publication
record. It accepts only an exact WP-1603 effect whose phase is `Completed`, binds the event to the
Fulfillment aggregate/version and final Handoff Record, and exposes only Fulfillment, Order,
Handoff Record, Store, verification-method category and completion time. Ordering is the declared
future consumer and remains responsible for its own workflow/projection.

No `PickupHandoffCompleted`, partial handoff event, recipient/customer data, raw proof, Ordering
mutation, consumer, Projection, HTTP/UI/Worker or runtime activation is introduced.

- Identity: `FulfillmentCompleted:v1`; producer/owner `@rms/fulfillment`; Store tenant scope;
  consumer `ordering.fulfillment-completed:v1`; replay idempotent; business-record retention.
- Envelope: canonical Event ID/type/version/time/producer/Tenant/Store/Aggregate/version,

- Payload: exact `fulfillmentReference`, `orderReference`, `handoffRecordReference`,
  `storeReference`, `verificationMethod` (`Opaque` or `HumanCode`) and `completedAt`.
