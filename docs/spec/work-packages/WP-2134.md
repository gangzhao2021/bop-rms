## Owning facts and boundaries

Procurement owns Purchase Order identity, scope, approval/issue facts, append-only issued snapshots,
post-Issue Revisions, Supplier acknowledgement/decline, cancellation and closure. A PO contains one
Supplier, one Buyer Legal Entity, one currency and one Ship-To Stock Site/address snapshot. It may
combine approved Requisition allocations but cannot create approval or Allocation facts on their
behalf. Inventory remains the sole actual receipt, Lot, Expiry, location and Stock Ledger owner.

## Acceptance contract

1. Model Brand-scoped Purchase Order as a stable Aggregate Root with independent Workflow,
   Fulfillment and Closure status dimensions, Expected Version and append-only decision history.
2. Enforce exactly one Supplier, Buyer Legal Entity, currency and Ship-To scope per PO. Validate the
   unique effective `Procurement Buyer` Operating Entity decision before Issue and pin its snapshot.
3. Require Offering-based lines with exact decimal ordered quantity/cost/discount/total, approved
   Offering/Version and Price Record/Version references, source Requisition allocations and complete
   Item/Supplier-item/unit/conversion/delivery/terms snapshots. Arbitrary line price is absent.
4. Keep approval separate from Issue. Enforce Draft edit, submit, segregated approve and explicit
   Issue Commands; only Issue creates the Supplier commitment and freezes the commercial snapshot.
5. Preserve every Issued/Acknowledged snapshot. Material quantity, cost, delivery or term changes
   create a post-Issue Revision with reason, independent submit/approval/issue and Supplier response;
   scope-identity changes require a new PO instead.
6. Record Supplier acknowledgement or decline separately. Cancellation never rewrites receipt facts;
   closure is allowed only after every line has a final received/cancelled outcome and no pending
   Revision or discrepancy.
7. Define strict `procurement_purchase_order_v1` list/editor/detail projection with Brand + Buyer
   Entity/Store scope, 30-second freshness, cursor pagination, registered filters, immutable source
   references and independently permission-trimmed cost, Supplier response, receipt/discrepancy and
   history fields.
8. Implement `PROC-PO-LIST`, `PROC-PO-EDITOR` and `PROC-PO-DETAIL` routes with registered fields,
   stale/partial read-only behavior, omitted unauthorized actions and adjacent blockers explaining
   approval, issue, Revision, acknowledgement, cancellation and closure requirements.
