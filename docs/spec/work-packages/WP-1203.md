## Purpose and boundaries

Attach one immutable Pricing Quote snapshot to the exact current Cart Version through an
Ordering-owned command. The command derives its complete Pricing request from the server-owned
Cart, accepts no client amount or Quote identity, requires complete WP-1202 Catalog evidence on
every Item, and verifies the returned Quote's Brand, Store, Cart, Cart Version, line identities,
Sellables, quantities, Catalog versions, Currency, totals, input digest and UTC validity before
persisting an append-only attachment.

Attaching a Quote does not mutate Cart contents or increment Cart Version. Later Item mutation
naturally makes an older attachment ineligible because it is pinned to the prior Cart Version.
Multiple immutable attachments may exist for requote history; no Quote or attachment is rewritten.

Out of scope: Pricing calculation implementation/private tables, client price, Promotion/Fee
authoring, Cart expiration (WP-1204), HTTP/PWA (WP-1205), Checkout confirmation (WP-1220), Order,
Payment, UI, Event, scheduler, real Store/price/tax facts and External Evidence.

## Adopted decisions and hard stops

1. Pricing owns Quote calculation and immutable Quote facts. Ordering consumes only the public
   `PriceQuoteSnapshot` contract through an injected application port and never reads Pricing
   persistence.
2. The Pricing request carries exact server-owned Cart scope and bounded Item/configuration
   evidence. Client amount, Currency, price, tax, expiry, Quote reference and input digest fields
   are impossible command inputs.
3. Empty Carts and legacy Items without complete Catalog selection evidence cannot be quoted.
4. Returned Quote scope and every line must exactly cover the Cart once. Missing, duplicate,
   foreign, stale, malformed, expired, future-created, blocked, inconsistent-Currency or
   arithmetically inconsistent results fail closed.
5. Exact UTC boundary applies: `requestedAt >= expiresAt` is expired. Warnings are retained;
   non-empty blockers prevent attachment.
6. Attachment is append-only and pinned to Quote Version, Cart Version, Quote input digest,
   Currency metadata version, totals, line Catalog versions, Quote creation/expiry and attach time.
7. A 24-hour idempotency record binds operation key, Guest Session and parsed intent. Exact replay
   returns the original attachment without reloading Cart or calling Pricing.
8. Authorization binds active Guest Session, Brand, Store and service mode; Audit contains no Cart
   note, amount breakdown or other sensitive free text.
9. Hard stop: foreign private-table access, client-controlled financial facts, cross-scope Quote,
   unchecked bigint/overflow, mutable history, sensitive telemetry, invented current business fact,
   or files outside the exact allowlist.
