# WP-2178 — Compliance Policy / Regulatory Requirement Version and Control Mapping

## Authority and scope

- Handoff Sections 40.5, 40.23–40.25, 40.29–40.31, 87.11.2, 88.16 and 88.21–88.30
  define versioned Compliance Policy / Regulatory Requirement configuration, non-weakenable hard
  requirements, review/publish controls and the canonical Screen contract.
- Exact local predecessor: verified WP-2177 commit `1aeeb1d`.
- Compliance owns Policy/Requirement definitions and immutable published Versions. Evidence owners
  retain Evidence; operational Domains retain their controls and enforcement outcomes. This WP
  stores only stable references, mappings and snapshots and never rewrites owner-Domain facts.
- Section 50 grants no Compliance schema or migration namespace. This WP adds no database object or
  runtime adapter and does not claim durable production operation.

## Acceptance contract

1. Stable Policy/Requirement identity is Tenant-bound and revisions pin Brand/Store applicability,
   jurisdiction, authority reference, requirement type, mandatory/advisory strength, applicable
   scope, IANA time zone, effective period, Evidence requirements, monitoring frequency, exact
   decimal threshold/limit, retention, escalation, notification requirement and Policy Version.
2. Draft revisions are append-only under exact expected version and idempotency. Published facts
   are immutable; changes create a new Version. Retiring a published Version is prospective and
   cannot rewrite Cases, Inspections or Monitoring Records that pinned the prior Version.
3. Platform/Brand/Store layering is explicit. A Brand or Store revision must reference its inherited
   parent Version and may add stricter controls only. It cannot reduce a Hard Requirement, widen an
   override, shorten mandatory retention, weaken Evidence/monitoring/notification or silently
   resolve uncertain legal interpretation.
4. Evidence and operational Control mappings contain stable owner references, required outcome and
   mapping status only. Compliance never embeds Evidence bytes, signed URLs, legal text or foreign
   mutable records and never executes the mapped owner Control.
5. Review is separated from authorship. `Legal Review Required` blocks publish until a distinct
   Counsel reviewer records a passed decision. Hard/high-risk Versions require a distinct second
   approver. Every publish re-authorizes and validates current mappings, effective period and parent.
6. Permission, purpose, Tenant/Brand/Store scope, exact expected version, audit context and
   idempotency are mandatory for every command, including idempotent replay. Restricted/free-form
   legal content, credentials, Customer/Employee identity and Provider payloads are excluded.
7. Section 40.29 defines no Policy event. This WP publishes no invented Domain Event; consumers pin
   exact Requirement Version through the public contract/query boundary.
8. `CMP-POLICY-LIST` at `/app/compliance/policies` and `CMP-POLICY-EDITOR` at
   `/app/compliance/policies/:id` expose canonical fields/actions plus explicit loading, denied,
   offline, stale, conflict, error and not-found states. Strict UI contracts accept safe codes,
   references, exact decimal strings and UTC/IANA values only.
