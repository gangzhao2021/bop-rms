## Implementation scope

Replace the loose pending key with a private foreground plan retaining original Cart identity/version,
operation key and unknown-outcome history. A retry cannot change its request or manufacture a new
key, including after refresh, a later rejection, reconnection or a superseded query. Preserve a
stable Cart snapshot and guard the plan's Session context before retry. Success alone resolves an
unknown plan; a first definitive rejection may release it. First version conflict retains the existing
fresh Cart query, but a later conflict after uncertainty cannot prove the original attempt failed.

Use query/command revisions to discard late loads so they cannot overwrite a newer command/result.
Loading while a Quote is in flight must not replace its plan or state. Refresh after an unknown
outcome may not silently switch the retry to another Cart/version. Offline does not allocate or
send new intent; reconnect restores appropriate controls without network replay. Keep coalescing
one in-flight Quote promise. Default operation keys retain actual UUIDv7 timestamp semantics.

The page hides new-Quote actions during unknown/pending/denied/offline states and disables offline
retry. Matching recovered states remain accessible, with no generic redesign. Existing repository
Playwright verification is the owning native-browser runner; add synthetic intercepted Entry/Cart/
Quote scenarios on the production bundle at desktop/narrow touch viewports. They prove foreground
controls and same-key retry, not real Store/Pricing/Provider or complete ordering readiness.
