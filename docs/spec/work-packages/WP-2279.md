# WP-2279 - Dining Admission Current Guest Binding

## Authority and readiness

Baseline b0eeb7862a221e7870d78274e99a3f7e536d4d7f; branch
codex/wp-2279-dining-admission-guest-binding. The Owner authorizes continuous bounded local
implementation and verified local commits. Composite Sections 0–97, accepted WP-1006 rules 17–21,
WP-2274 current Guest authority and WP-2278 Guest-bound original Join history govern this correction.
Existing GuestDiningAdmissionPort.consume receives only admission/operation/time; its returned
GuestDiningAdmissionEvidence carries no Guest identity. Before durable consumption is connected,
this boundary must identify and verify the exact current Guest Session whose Join issued admission.

## Bounded correction

Require guestSessionReference in the internal admission-consumption command and in its closed
Allowed evidence. GuestSessionService.bindDining derives the value from its validated current
ContextOnly DineIn Guest record, passes it to the owning admission port and rejects evidence for
another Guest or without the field before rotating/persisting credentials. Client input cannot
choose the Guest reference. Missing, malformed or wrong-reference evidence fails closed; no legacy
fallback treats an admission locator as a grant. Existing current version, Store, public Table,
operation and expiry checks remain in force. Exact successful binding replay behavior is retained.
