## Owning facts and boundaries

Recipe owns versioned Recipes, Ingredient Requirements, Yield / loss, Sub-recipe bindings,
measurement conversions, Preparation Steps, theoretical Inventory consumption, cost and structured
Allergen source union, SKU / Option resolution, Store overrides and publication lifecycle. Catalog,
Inventory and Pricing supply public references or version-pinned evidence through ports; Recipe does
not query their private tables or copy Product, SKU, Inventory Item, stock, Supplier or price facts.

Allergen and nutrition facts are evidence-backed structured references, never medical advice or
developer-authored legal conclusions. Real supplier documents, professional reviewers, cost facts
and Inventory mappings are External Evidence and are not invented by this WP.

## Acceptance contract

1. Create / replace / publish / invalidate / archive operations require Brand scope, Actor, purpose,
   `recipe.manage`, Expected Version, idempotency, public-fact validation, append-only Audit and
   atomic minimal Events.
2. Publish requires two distinct authorized reviewers: one for cost / operational correctness and
   one for food-safety / Allergen evidence; neither may be the Draft author.
3. Exact decimal quantities, unit conversions, Yield and loss calculations are deterministic;
   incompatible dimensions, missing Inventory mapping, unresolved source, direct / indirect Recipe
   cycles or unbounded graphs fail closed.
4. Published Parents pin Sub-recipe and conversion versions. Invalidation creates a new lifecycle
   fact and impact summary; it never mutates historical Order / Kitchen snapshots.
5. `RECIPE-LIST` and `RECIPE-EDITOR` implement every Section 88 field, filter, action and mandatory
   state; without an authorized BFF mutations are visibly disabled.
