## Owning facts and boundaries

Inventory owns Goods Receipt identity, actual accepted/rejected/damaged quantities, lot/expiry,
location, quality/temperature evidence and immutable Stock Ledger movements. Procurement supplies an
issued PO/line snapshot and owns only its event-derived fulfillment projection. Neither side reads
the other's private tables. Posted receipt errors are corrected only by an Inventory Adjustment or
explicit receipt Adjustment/Void fact; Procurement never accepts a manual received-quantity write.

## Acceptance contract

1. Model Stock-Site-scoped Goods Receipt as an Inventory Aggregate with Draft validation and one
   immutable Posted fact. Capture Supplier/PO/Revision/line snapshot references and actual Item,
   lot/expiry/location, exact quantities/unit, received time, quality/temperature evidence and Actor.
2. Validate exact Tenant, Brand, Stock Site, PO/version/issued snapshot, line, Item, purchase/base
   unit conversion and cumulative quantities against a current public Procurement receiving snapshot.
3. Enforce `Block`, `ManagerOverride` or `AllowWithinTolerance` over-receipt policy. Every overage,
   shortage, rejection or damage is explicit; override binds reason, independent approval and Actor.
4. Post one atomic immutable Receipt plus accepted Stock Movements. Rejected/damaged quantity never
   enters accepted Stock; duplicate operation/Event IDs replay one outcome and partial failure leaves
   neither Receipt nor Movement committed.
5. Append explicit receipt Adjustment/Void facts instead of editing/deleting the original. Preserve
   correction chain, evidence and exact compensating Stock Movement references.
6. Emit strict public `GoodsReceiptPosted`, `GoodsReceiptAdjusted` and `GoodsReceiptVoided` contracts
   with Event/Receipt/line/PO refs, exact accepted/rejected/damaged quantities, unit, Stock Site and
   receipt time. Procurement consumes idempotently and cannot override the cumulative result.
7. Define strict `inventory_goods_receipt_v1` projection with explicit Stock Site, PO/Supplier/Item/
   barcode search, permission-trimmed cost/evidence/temperature, current freshness and partial state.
8. Implement canonical `INV-GOODS-RECEIPT` `/operations/receiving/new` wizard with PO/line snapshot,
   lot/expiry/location, quality/temperature/variance/tolerance review, quarantine/discrepancy cues,
   immutable submit confirmation, stale/offline read-only handling and omitted unauthorized actions.
