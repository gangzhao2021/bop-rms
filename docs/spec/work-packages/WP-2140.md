## Owning facts and boundaries

Customer & Loyalty owns the Brand-specific Customer Profile and its relationship facts. Identity
owns authentication and real Email/Phone values. Order and Reservation own Guest contact snapshots,
participants and historical transactions. Linking appends a Customer-owned association and never
rewrites a transaction, Guest Session, participant or Audit Actor.

## Acceptance contract

1. Create a Profile only for registration/login, Loyalty enrollment, explicit retention consent or
   authorized Staff business need, preserving exact source and evidence.
2. Store verified Contact References only after current Identity proof; never store passwords or raw
   Email/Phone. Linking a User requires verified Identity evidence.
3. Link an eligible Guest Order/Reservation idempotently only after source-owner validation binds
   Tenant, Brand, transaction, Guest identity and verification proof; never infer by matching contact.
4. Preserve append-only relationship decisions, transaction links and service notes with Expected
   Version, Actor, purpose, reason, idempotency and Audit.
5. Define strict `customer_profile_v1` list/detail reads with purpose authorization and separately
   trimmed contact, transaction, loyalty, consent, communication, privacy, notes and audit fields.
6. Implement `CRM-CUSTOMER-LIST` `/app/customers` and `CRM-CUSTOMER-DETAIL`
   `/app/customers/:id`, all registered states, stale/offline read-only and no PII in URLs/analytics.
