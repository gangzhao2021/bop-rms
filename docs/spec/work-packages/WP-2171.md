## Acceptance contract

1. A strict Compliance Case snapshot pins stable Case identity, Tenant/Brand/optional Store,
   canonical Type, Severity, Lifecycle, owner/deadline/timezone, primary/supporting opaque scopes,
   versioned Requirement references and immutable revision/version metadata.
2. Commands open, assign, transition, escalate, cancel and close by exact expected version and
   idempotency key. Closed Cases never reopen; cancellation requires a bounded reason code; close
   requires owner-issued gate evidence for Critical Findings, Mandatory Corrective Actions,
   Evidence, reinspection, Regulatory Notification and linked Domain actions.
3. Containment records only an authorized requirement and linked owning-Domain outcome for the
   closed `StopSelling | StopProduction | StopReceiving | HoldInventory | DisableEquipment |
SuspendDeliveryPickup | StoreEmergencyClosure` vocabulary. Compliance never reports Applied or
   Released without an opaque owner-issued Action/Event reference; Immediate Danger/Hard Block
   release requires verified evidence and is not Manager-overridable.
4. Regulatory Notification preserves Requirement Version, Authority, deadline, status, immutable
   Submission revision/reference and acknowledgement/follow-up codes. Deadline passage never
   implies submission; `NotRequired` requires an exact basis code.
5. Every mutation revalidates Tenant Context, Actor, purpose, action-specific permission, exact
   scope, expected version, idempotency and safe Audit evidence. Dependency uncertainty, stale
   versions, cross-scope references and malformed/restricted content fail closed.
6. `CMP-CASE-LIST` and `CMP-CASE-DETAIL` expose the Section 88 fields, filters, action gating and
   explicit loading/denied/offline/stale/conflict/error/empty states. UI contracts accept no raw
   Evidence, narratives, health/allergy/contact data, authority payload, legal advice or arbitrary
   URLs/free text.
7. Case revisions, containment/notification records and Events are append-only public contracts.
   Merge/split remain review/navigation intent until a later accepted contract resolves legal
   entity/authority constraints; no history deletion or source-Domain rewrite is permitted.
