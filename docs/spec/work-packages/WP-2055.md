## Resolved local boundary

WP-2055 adds a deterministic fail-closed validator for the complete statutory-record lifecycle. It
separates recovery backups from the legal archive; requires minimized finalized business facts,
linked correction/refund/void records, fiscal-year-end retention, legal-hold precedence, privileged
audited access, daily source verification, quarterly cross-Tenant-safe reconstruction and privacy
tombstone replay before traffic.

Synthetic references prove validator behavior only. The WP does not approve legal retention, enable
irreversible Object Lock Compliance mode, access production records or perform a real deletion.
