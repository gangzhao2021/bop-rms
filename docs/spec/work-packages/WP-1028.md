## Purpose

Implement the smallest provider-neutral Pilot allergen safety contract that proves:

1. every allergen registry、Ingredient、Recipe、Product and Option source is version-pinned；
2. each source distinguishes `Contains`、`CrossContactPossible` and `Unverified`；
3. missing、unknown、expired、invalidated or unresolved source evidence blocks Menu review/publication；
4. the Sellable disclosure is the deterministic union of the base path and every enabled Option
   path, with `Contains` stronger than cross-contact；
5. missing data never implies absence and removing an Ingredient or Option never creates an
   allergen-free claim；
6. customer Menu DTOs expose only approved localized disclosure plus the controlled Staff
   assistance path；and
7. evidence and derived disclosure persistence is append-only and Brand RLS-scoped.

## Adopted boundaries

- Catalog owns Ingredient/allergen declaration and provenance facts. Compliance verifies policy
  and operations; WP-1407 and WP-2027 own structured Staff assistance、KDS acknowledgement、
  payment blocking and incident end-to-end behavior.
- The Canada registry and policy document are versioned references and digests. This package does
  not invent legal copy or claim that a real policy、supplier document、Recipe or reviewer exists.
- `Unverified` remains a valid stored source classification so uncertainty is explicit, but it can
  never produce passing publication evidence.
- The base plus Option union retains cross-contact cues and emits `allergenFreeClaim: false` plus
  `ALLERGEN_ASSISTANCE_REQUIRED` on every public disclosure.
- Validation evidence is bound to the exact Brand、Menu Version、snapshot digest and earliest
  source expiry, then composes the existing WP-1024 Submit Review gate.
- Customer filtering by allergen and accommodation workflows are excluded. No free-text medical
  narrative、automated safety decision、UI or External Evidence is added.

## Acceptance

- verified base plus Option assertions produce deterministic localized disclosure；
- `Contains` dominates `CrossContactPossible` for the same allergen without deleting the warning；
- unknown、expired、invalidated、duplicate or unresolved evidence fails closed；
- exact validation evidence unlocks only the matching Menu snapshot Submit Review transition；
- public DTOs never promise allergen absence and always expose the controlled assistance code；
- persisted policy/source versions、digests and validation results cannot be updated or deleted；
  and
- cross-Brand reads are denied until exact Tenant Context is set.
