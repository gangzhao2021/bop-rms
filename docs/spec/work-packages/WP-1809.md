## Ownership and execution contract

The generated `@bop/projection` module is technical contract owner of rebuildable cross-Domain
operational views. Section 92 keeps `platform_projection` reserved; a later persistence WP must use
explicit `projection-builder` access rather than Module schema ownership. This WP owns no source
business fact and adds a strict contract-first
Order Exception projection over Dining unpaid Batch, Payment reconciliation difference, capture
deadline exceeded and paid-without-fulfillable Order sources. It accepts only safe Store-scoped
references, bounded state, version/digest and canonical instants—never amount, Provider IDs/payloads,
Customer/contact, actor details or free text.

Critical sources must be projected within 15 minutes or activation fails with `SLA_MISSED`;
cross-scope facts fail closed. Rebuild is deterministic from immutable source contracts. No database
table/migration is added in this contract-first slice; durable consumer/checkpoint/storage remains
External Evidence.

> 2026-10-17 note (WP-2423, DEC-EXCEPTION-CONFIRMED-REFUND-DUE; supersedes the row due time
> above for this case only, Owner may revisit): the 15-minute visibility SLA is unchanged. A row's
> due time stays 15 minutes after it opened, except an open `PaidWithoutFulfillableOrder` whose
> refund the Provider has already confirmed: it only awaits the Store's reconciliation check and is
> due at the end of the Store business day (Store time zone and business-day start) it opened in,
> shown to staff as "Refunded — check by end of day"; it is overdue only after that. The workbench
> read lists every open row on each read (not a lifetime-bounded scan) and pages resolved rows.

Actions are intent routing only. Acknowledge/assign/resolve route to Task; compensation/retry route
to Payment; write-off request routes to Ordering. Every action requires exact Actor permission,
source version and idempotency reference. Resolve requires owning-source final evidence. Projection,
UI and Provider pending/unknown state never become financial or lifecycle finality.
