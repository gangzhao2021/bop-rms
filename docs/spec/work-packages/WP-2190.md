# WP-2190 — Operating Entity, Business Function and Authority Administration

## Authority and scope

- Handoff Sections 42.5, 48.3.4, 52–58, 86.3/86.23, 87.5–87.7 and
  88.7/88.21–88.30 define the Operating Entity owner, Ontario-first Pilot constraint, controlled
  references, approval boundary and canonical screens.
- Exact local predecessor: verified WP-2183 commit `2d47960`.
- `@bop/operating-entity` owns Operating Entity identity, versioned legal/operating profile metadata,
  effective Business Function assignment and authority-summary references. Tenant owns Brand/Store
  scope; Permission owns authorization; Approval/Audit own decisions/evidence; Compliance owns
  licence Cases; Tax/Payment/Procurement own their calculations and transactions.

## Acceptance contract

1. Preserve the existing independent Operating Entity Aggregate and Ontario Pilot constraints. The
   implementation creates no corporation, filing, registration number, tax identifier, bank account,
   officer identity, address, licence, Provider account or legal conclusion.
2. Versioned administration stores legal/trade name, jurisdiction, registration/tax/billing/
   settlement/registered-address references, evidence references and field classification only.
   Restricted identifiers and personal details stay behind opaque references and never enter list
   search, logs, URLs, analytics, fixtures or unrestricted projections.
3. Authority summaries are append-only, effective-dated, role-coded references. Restricted reveal
   requires exact permission, declared purpose and server-validated recent MFA; this WP exposes no
   significant-control register, raw filing or personal address.
4. Draft revision, submit, independent approve/reject, activate, suspend and effective Brand/Store
   Business Function assignment require exact scope, expected version, idempotency, purpose and
   append-only Audit/operation evidence. Submitter cannot approve; assignment overlap remains a
   fail-closed conflict and every Store + Business Function + effective instant resolves uniquely.
5. Public query contract `operating_entity_admin_v1` and `ORG-ENTITY-LIST`
   `/app/organization/entities` plus `ORG-ENTITY-DETAIL` `/app/organization/entities/:id` expose
   only field-masked metadata, bounded exact registration search when authorized, status/
   jurisdiction filters, Brand/Store counts, assignments, safe authority summaries, history and
   evidence references. Loading/empty/denied/not-found/feature-disabled/offline/stale/conflict/error
   states and responsive/accessibility behavior are explicit.
6. This WP creates no external filing, professional approval, real Pilot entity, production
   activation, runtime role/grant, legal/tax/payment calculation, Provider integration, unrestricted
   export or new Event. Those remain gated by accepted External Evidence and owner-Domain contracts.
