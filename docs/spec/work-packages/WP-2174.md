# WP-2174 — Permit, Employee / Supplier / Device Qualification and Expiry

## Authority and scope

- Handoff Sections 31.18, 36.2–36.6, 40.1, 40.6, 40.15–40.16, 40.23–40.25,
  40.28–40.29 and 88.16 define qualification ownership, immutable records, expiry, safe
  cross-Domain restrictions and the canonical screen.
- Exact local predecessor: verified WP-2173 commit `9443308`.
- Compliance owns License / Permit and Employee Qualification records. Procurement owns Supplier
  Qualification inside the Supplier Aggregate; Device owns Device identity, lifecycle, capability
  and health. Compliance may retain immutable verification/requirement assessments and opaque
  owning-Domain outcome references, but never rewrites Supplier, Employee, Membership or Device.
- Section 50 grants no Compliance schema or migration namespace. This WP adds no database object or
  runtime adapter and does not claim durable production operation.

## Acceptance contract

1. License / Permit records pin exact Tenant/Brand/optional Store scope, holder Legal Entity,
   License Type, Authority, number reference, issued/effective/expiry instants, renewal window,
   Requirement Version, Evidence reference, Verification result and immutable revision.
2. License status is closed to `Draft | PendingVerification | Active | Expiring | Expired |
Suspended | Revoked | Closed`. Renewal creates a new Record or revision while preserving prior
   facts. `LicenseExpiring`, `LicenseExpired` and `LicenseSuspended` describe only committed facts.
3. Employee Qualification records pin Employee/Membership reference, qualification type,
   jurisdiction, certificate reference, issuer, effective/expiry, Requirement Version, Evidence
   reference and Verified At/By. New certificates create new Records and publish only
   `EmployeeQualificationExpiring` or `EmployeeQualificationExpired` when those facts commit.
4. Supplier and Device assessment records pin the owner Record reference/version and requirement
   result. Supplier qualification source facts remain Procurement-owned and Device identity,
   capability, health and calibration source facts remain Device-owned. Compliance records only
   an immutable assessment and opaque owning-Domain eligibility outcome reference.
5. Qualification status is closed to `Draft | PendingVerification | Active | Expiring | Expired |
Suspended | Revoked | Closed`; Verification result is closed to `Pending | Verified | Rejected |
Unverified`. Status transitions, expiry evaluation and renewal never rewrite historical records.
6. Expiry can request a Renewal Task and eligibility suspension only through explicit ports with
   authorized, idempotent, scope-pinned outcomes. It never directly changes scheduling, access,
   Offering/PO, Device routing, Order, Production or Store operation, and never rewrites history.
7. Every mutation revalidates Tenant Context, purpose, action-specific Permission, exact scope,
   expected revision, idempotency and safe Audit evidence. Cross-scope or stale owner facts,
   inconsistent effective periods, restricted content and dependency uncertainty fail closed.
8. `CMP-QUALIFICATION` exposes Section 88 fields, filters, permission-gated actions and explicit
   loading/denied/offline/stale/conflict/error/empty states. Its strict UI contract accepts safe
   codes/references/counts/statuses only and never raw Evidence, certificate numbers, employee PII,
   credentials, legal narrative, arbitrary free text or URLs.
