## Owning facts and boundaries

Procurement owns the derived Supplier Performance projection. Purchase Order, Revision, Supplier
Acknowledgement and Inventory Goods Receipt Events remain immutable source facts. Recalculation
never writes those facts or Supplier lifecycle. Manual Assessment is an independent append-only
record; a low score may open a review task but cannot suspend or deactivate a Supplier.

## Acceptance contract

1. Derive on-time, fill, accepted-quality, discrepancy frequency, acknowledgement response,
   decline/cancellation and purchase-price-variance metrics from exact public facts.
2. Calculate quantities and rates with integer/fixed-decimal arithmetic, preserve numerator,
   denominator, definition version, period, source counts and incomplete coverage.
3. Scope by Tenant, Brand, optional Stock Site, Supplier, Offering, item category and UTC interval;
   authorize before any source or projection read.
4. Define strict `procurement_supplier_performance_v1` with freshness, partial/rebuild state and
   independently trimmed fact drill, price variance and manual assessment fields.
5. Append a Manual Assessment without changing system metrics, or explicitly open a public review
   task through idempotent, audited handoff. No threshold automatically mutates Supplier state.
6. Implement `SUP-PERFORMANCE` `/app/supply/performance` with registered filters, coverage, fact
   drill, authorized export and review-task action; stale/offline remains read-only.
