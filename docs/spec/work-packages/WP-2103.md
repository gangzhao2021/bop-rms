## Owning facts and evidence boundary

Pricing continues to own versioned Store Tax Configuration, exact tax calculation and receipt
presentation codes. Store and Operating Entity supply public Store/jurisdiction/registration
references; Catalog supplies public tax-classification references. This WP does not author a legal
rate, classification, registration, rebate or receipt conclusion.

`SPIKE-1106` professional review remains a publish gate. Drafts may be incomplete, but Publish
requires verified registration applicability, exact snapshot-bound professional approval, an
approved fixture-suite reference/digest, complete required-context coverage and passing Basket and
Refund fixtures. Repository tests use explicitly synthetic values only.

## Public contract

- Commands: create professional-fixture-backed Draft, replace Draft and four-eyes Publish.
- Writes require Tenant/Brand/Store, Actor, purpose, `pricing.tax-config.manage`, Expected Version,
  idempotency, public-fact validation, append-only Audit and atomic Event persistence. Publish also
  requires `pricing.tax-config.approve` by an Actor other than the Draft author.
- Events: `TaxConfigDraftCreated:v1`, `TaxConfigDraftReplaced:v1` and `TaxConfigPublished:v1`, with
  minimal Store-scoped payloads and replay-safe `pricing.tax-config-admin-projection:v1` consumers.
- Queries: `query.tax_config`, `pricing_tax_config_admin_v1`, rule/receipt fixture rows and a
  generation checkpoint. Projection data is rebuildable and forced-RLS Store scoped.

## Simulation and screen contract

Approved fixture simulation resolves the exact published snapshot, calculates signed integer minor
units, supports Basket and symmetric Refund paths, preserves component/rate/treatment/version
explanations and produces a receipt preview. Fixture reference and digest must match the exact
professional evidence; arbitrary operator-entered fixtures cannot become approval evidence.

`TAX-CONFIG` at `/app/commerce/tax` exposes Store registration applicability, tax categories,
rates/inclusive flag, jurisdiction, effective dates, fixture status, category/code search,
jurisdiction/active/future filters, Basket/Refund simulation, receipt preview and submit/approve/
publish actions. It implements Loading, Empty, Permission Denied, Not Found, Feature Disabled,
Stale, Conflict, Command Failed and Offline Read-only states. Without an authorized BFF, mutations
are visibly disabled.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Draft/Publish operations enforce exact Store scope, permission, Expected Version, idempotency,
   Audit, four-eyes approval and atomic minimal Events.
2. Publish fails closed without professional evidence, registration applicability, complete
   classification coverage or a passing exact approved fixture suite.
3. Basket and Refund simulations use version-pinned rules and exact signed integer-minor-unit math;
   receipt preview retains component explanation without asserting a legal conclusion.
4. Rebuildable Tax Admin projection persistence enforces exact minor units, forced Store RLS and no
   PUBLIC grants.
5. `TAX-CONFIG` exposes every registered field/action/state and rejects markup, URLs and numeric
   money from untrusted projections.
