## Owning facts and boundary

Catalog continues to own `RMS-CAT-AVAILABILITY-RULE` as Brand-scoped Configuration. WP-2101
extends the accepted SKU-only overlay to Product, SKU and Bundle targets and adds the management
projection and Screen contract. Inventory balance, Store identity/time-zone configuration and Kill
Switch state remain external public facts; Catalog neither copies private tables nor infers them.

Rule lifecycle remains `Draft | Active | Inactive | Archived`. Exact Store scope outranks Brand
defaults, then priority resolves within the winning scope. Equal-priority conflicting decisions,
missing rules and invalid/stale safety evidence fail closed as `Indeterminate`. UTC instants are
stored; simulation also requires an explicit IANA time zone and matching Business Date.

## Public contract

- Commands: `CreateAvailabilityRule`, `ReplaceAvailabilityRule`, `ScheduleAvailabilityRule`,
  `PauseAvailabilityRule`, `ResumeAvailabilityRule`, and reason-required Kill Switch intent through
  its owning safety boundary.
- Writes require Tenant/Brand, Actor, purpose, `catalog.availability.manage`, Expected Version where
  applicable, idempotency reference, exact public facts, UTC request time and append-only Audit.
- Events: `AvailabilityRuleCreated:v1`, `AvailabilityRuleReplaced:v1` and
  `AvailabilityRuleLifecycleChanged:v1`, committed atomically with the operation/Audit adapter and
  consumed idempotently by `catalog.availability-workbench-projection:v1`.
- Query/projection: `query.cat_availability` and `catalog_availability_workbench_v1`; generation,
  row and checkpoint persistence makes replay/rebuild explicit and Brand RLS enforced.
- Stable invalid-input, permission, version/idempotency/code/lifecycle and dependency-unavailable
  errors remain safe. Unknown facts never become “available”.

## Screen contract

`CAT-AVAILABILITY` at `/app/commerce/availability` exposes item/code search; Store, source,
lifecycle and unavailable filters; rule/scope/Store/channel/schedule/source/priority/effective
result/reason fields; and create/edit/simulate/schedule/pause/resume/Kill Switch actions. Loading,
empty, permission denied, not found, feature disabled, stale, conflict, command failed and offline
read-only states are explicit. Mutations and simulation are visibly disabled until an authorized BFF
adapter is connected; client state is never presented as an accepted Catalog fact.

Section 88.4 and 88.27–88.30 apply: semantic labels/landmarks, keyboard-equivalent controls,
visible focus, non-color status, reflow/zoom/localization/reduced-motion support and minimum touch
targets. Analytics contains only allowlisted Screen/action/result codes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Strict parsing accepts Product/SKU/Bundle, rejects unknown targets and invalid effective periods.
2. Effective simulation requires exact Store/channel/order type, UTC instant, IANA time zone and
   matching Business Date and preserves the accepted safety-first fail-closed resolution.
3. Create/replace/lifecycle writes retain authorization, Expected Version, idempotency, fact
   validation, append-only Audit and atomic event behavior.
4. Persistence safely expands existing SKU rows, supports polymorphic targets, owns a rebuildable
   projection and enforces forced Brand RLS with no PUBLIC grants.
5. `CAT-AVAILABILITY` implements the registered fields, filters, actions and nine states with
   synthetic privacy-safe tests.
