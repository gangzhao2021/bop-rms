## Resolved local boundary

WP-2053 adds a deterministic fail-closed validator for one disaster-recovery evidence bundle. It
requires the accepted Canada cross-Region topology, encrypted Multi-AZ/PITR and replica controls,
five-minute transaction RPO, sixty-minute RTO, write fencing, exact recovered source facts,
privacy-tombstone reapplication, Provider reconciliation, DNS authority and measured drill evidence.

Synthetic evidence proves validator behavior only. This WP does not create or promote an RDS
replica, copy production data, mutate DNS, access credentials or claim that a real recovery drill ran.

## Acceptance map

1. Exact `ca-central-1` primary and `ca-west-1` recovery profile, encryption, Multi-AZ, PITR,
   cross-Region automated backups and 35-day retention are mandatory.
2. Replica evidence proves read-only-before-promotion and accepted 2-minute alert / 5-minute page
   thresholds; measured lag above the 5-minute RPO fails closed.
3. S3/ECR replication and destination preparation prohibit unnecessary PII copies.
4. Failover fences primary writes, prevents split brain, records the last confirmed position,
   promotes the destination, rotates both endpoints and preserves Idempotency Keys.
5. Restore includes source transactions, pg-boss, Outbox, Inbox, Idempotency, Session revocation and
   Audit; projections/caches rebuild and never define the recovery point.
6. Traffic remains fenced until schema, Audit chain, malware/object-reference, authorization and
   privacy-tombstone checks pass, with isolated-restore destruction evidence.
7. Payment, webhook and email reconcile before Unknown operations retry.
8. Route 53 evidence names authority, health, communications and rollback/fail-forward criteria.
9. Quarterly restore and semiannual failover evidence stay within 5-minute RPO / 60-minute RTO.
10. Missing, malformed, unsupported or out-of-objective evidence fails closed without a production
    SLO claim.
