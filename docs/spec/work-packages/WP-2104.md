## Owning facts and boundaries

Pricing owns versioned Promotion eligibility, Benefit, limits, stacking/priority, budget admission,
simulation and selection explanation. Catalog supplies public Product/Category/Sellable references;
Customer may supply a PII-free segment reference or verified Coupon entitlement. Pricing never
reads their private tables. Bundle base/upgrade pricing and Loyalty redemption are not Promotions.

Supported types follow Section 27.5: Item/Order percentage or fixed discount, threshold benefit,
Buy X Get Y, Happy Hour, Coupon and authorized Manual Discount. Every Promotion declares
`Exclusive`, `SameGroupExclusive` or `Stackable`. Incompatible eligible Promotions resolve to the
greatest deterministic customer saving and retain reasons for unselected candidates.

## Acceptance contract

1. Draft/Publish/Pause/Archive commands require Brand scope, Actor, purpose, explicit Promotion
   permission, Expected Version, idempotency, public-fact validation, append-only Audit and atomic
   minimal Events; Publish requires a distinct authorized approver.
2. Benefit math uses exact integer minor units and canonical decimal percentages. Discounts cannot
   make a line or basket negative, overflow or cross Currency.
3. Budget and usage facts are explicit; concurrent admission is an atomic persistence responsibility
   and simulation never reserves/consumes budget.
4. Publish fails closed for circular Benefit references, ambiguous stacking, invalid/overlapping
   schedules, missing approval, PII-bearing segment/customer copy or unbounded limits.
5. `PROMO-LIST` and `PROMO-EDITOR` implement every Section 88 field, action and mandatory state;
   without an authorized BFF mutations are visibly disabled.
