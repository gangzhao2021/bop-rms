## Resolved local boundary

WP-2066 adds a deterministic fail-closed evidence contract for reviewed CloudFormation change sets,
weekly drift detection, production termination/deletion protection, recovery-copy policy and fenced
cross-account restore verification. Unexplained drift creates an owned expiring exception and is
never silently auto-reconciled. Restore remains isolated from Customer/Provider traffic until all
schema, Audit, object, authorization and privacy-tombstone checks pass.

The local contract composes the existing disaster-recovery and statutory-archive gates. It stores no
stack/account/backup identifier, credential, production data or claimed AWS/drill result.
