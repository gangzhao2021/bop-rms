## Acceptance and design

Add a private foreground journey composing the Join client and internally constructed existing binding
coordinator. Start accepts closed operationReference/joinCredential; resume accepts only the original
operationReference. Capture own data and current opaque CSRF generation. Single-flight same intent;
no retargeting unresolved intent. Other starts require a separately established journey, not implicit
replacement. No browser storage, background retry, Cookie manipulation or success inferred offline.

Before a validated Join receipt, resume sends the original Join operation/credential. After exact
Joined operation/admission validation, erase raw Join credential and move to binding permanently.
Generate one distinct binding logical operation; delegate preparation/activation/recovery to existing
binding coordinator. Never repeat Join after admission is known. Repeat completed resume performs
current binding completion, not cached authority. Expose only Bound/original operation to callers.

Validate opaque generation and exact current CSRF before/after asynchronous results and final delivery.
Wrap the internally owned binding coordinator's verified CSRF publication to record only that known
rotation. Do not blindly recapture context after awaits or adopt an external reset, including same-value
or ABA changes. All failures are bounded; stale/unknown cannot publish success or new credentials.

Actual PostgreSQL/HTTP synthetic journey must lose Join acknowledgement, recover original admission,
then lose binding activation acknowledgement; resume completes the original preparation even after its
expiry, without another Join. Retain one Join Audit, one consumption and three Identity preparation
revisions/Audit. No browser/TLS/production Store claim. UI and replacement/reload recovery remain separate.
