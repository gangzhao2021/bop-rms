owner validity, with no production default. Generate fresh candidate Session/CSRF and recovery
proof, store hashes only, return credentials solely for future no-store same-origin transport.

exact candidate possession, consumes the same Dining admission through the existing public
GuestDiningAdmissionPort, validates the pure activation plan and persists the exact candidate.
Unknown owner/Identity outcomes cannot report success. Original retry after owner failure or
Identity rollback retains the same candidate; completion after Identity commit authenticates
the already-delivered DiningBound candidate/CSRF, never revoked predecessor or consumed proof.

Finish checks the complete Identity receipt and exact Dining binding. Current Dining authority
remains supplied by the authorization binding port. No extra owner activation is required after
Dining admission consumption. Recovery HMAC has a dedicated Dining purpose distinct from Pickup,
Session and CSRF. No raw secrets enter owner requests/history/errors.
