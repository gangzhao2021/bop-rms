## Contract and ownership

Ordering remains owner of Order identity, number, type/channel, phase and submission time. Payment,
Kitchen and Fulfillment remain owners of their own summaries. `@bop/projection` consumes only strict
Store-scoped public snapshots, never private tables. A row with no collaborating source explicitly
reports `NotReported` or `Unavailable`; Payment `Unknown` is retained rather than guessed.

Every generation declares Tenant/Brand/Store, Business Date, version, generation/checkpoint,
`asOfUtc`, `projectedAt`, `lastRebuiltAt`, freshness and initialized-empty. Duplicate sources,
cross-scope facts and collaborating rows without an Ordering source fail closed. The 2-second live
target determines Fresh/Stale; stale rows are readable but cannot authorize mutation. Rebuild uses
the same pure builder and source checkpoint. Shadow persistence/version switch remains WP-1904.
